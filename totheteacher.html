To the Teacher

These materials were originally targeted to teachers. Only later was material added so that a student could work through it on his own.

Much of this material is mainstream. I have tried to stay as close to the curriculum of the Sutherland Cranial Teaching Foundation and Cranial Academy as possible, and many of my techniques are from Magoun’s “Osteopathy in the Cranial Field”. Other techniques I have developed myself over the years that are also good teaching tools are also included. I hope you will find these useful.

The landmark sections will support a variety of techniques in a given area. For the technique itself, have the student read about it beforehand, and then you can spend more time on palpation. Long-term student retention will also be improved.

If you want to play part of a video during a teaching session, I have included a complete set of instructions for every presentation. So even though it may be 1 in a series of progressively more difficult exercises, it is still a stand-alone video.
